Charging Station App

2024 · Delta-N internship · low-code

Role: Solo developer

Delta-N had charging stations but no way to reserve them. I proposed a fix and built a low-code app: see which stations are free, pick a time slot and reserve it in seconds.

How it works:

  1. Check availability: free stations show green, taken ones red, in real time.
  2. Pick a time: choose the date, start time and end time in a simple form.
  3. Reserve: one tap and the reservation is confirmed.

Result: Replaced walking-and-hoping with reservations; the app is still used daily.

Youth Voting Platform

2023 · Municipality of Delft · solo, age 15

Role: Solo developer & designer

An event-ideas mailbox for young people in Delft. I wrote the project plan, designed the interface, built it in Oracle APEX and wrote the email logic in PL/SQL: every submission sends one email to the municipality and one back to the young person.

How it works:

  1. Submit an idea: a four-step form with name, age, contact details, target group and the event idea.
  2. Review: a summary of everything before submitting.
  3. Back-end: two automatic emails per submission, written in PL/SQL.

Result: Delivered to the Municipality of Delft as a working platform.
